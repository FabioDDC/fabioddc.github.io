LeaseLock

A smart lock for renters. It clamps onto the deadbolt's existing thumb-turn, so the lock itself never changes, and locks the door automatically when it closes. Scroll to see the parts inside.

Sensing

Reed switch and magnet

Tell the Arduino when the door is closed.

Brain

Arduino Uno

Waits for the door to close, then runs the motor.

Driver

L298N motor driver

Turns the Arduino's signals into motor power.

Motion

NEMA 17 stepper motor

Spins the worm gear when the door closes.

Locking

Worm gear and beam clamp

Turn the thumb-turn to lock the deadbolt. Keep scrolling to lock it.

Power

9 V battery

Powers the motor driver, which also feeds the Arduino.
